# Chapter 4.5 — The corporate dashboard: Hiring Path, Parallel Tracks, and Supporting

You've finished organisation setup (Chapter 3) and the trial-and-billing logistics are in your head (Chapter 4). Before you start posting requisitions, take ten minutes to understand the dashboard layout. The Business surface of Sebenza Hub has roughly 50 pages organised into a 6-step Hiring Path and five Parallel Tracks — knowing how they're grouped will save you hours of hunting.

![[c-ch045-corporate-dashboard-full-view.png]]

## The three sections of the sidebar

The left sidebar is divided into three sections, in this order from top to bottom:

1. **Hiring Path** — the linear journey from organisation setup to a signed offer.
2. **Parallel Tracks** — the five "always-on" sets of tools that support the path.
3. **Supporting** — the knowledge base and the admin pages (billing, settings, SSO).

A thick visual divider separates the Hiring Path from the Parallel Tracks. This is intentional. The Hiring Path is what you walk through to make a hire; the Parallel Tracks are tools you reach for at specific moments.

## The Hiring Path (in order)

The path follows the actual sequence of work in a corporate TA function:

| Step | Sidebar group | What you do here |
|------|---------------|------------------|
| 1 | Organization | The dashboard home, plus the team you've invited |
| 2 | Post Jobs | Create and publish requisitions |
| 3 | Receive Applications | Triage what's landed: applications, pipeline, candidate database |
| 4 | Screen & Evaluate | Configure screening criteria, run the bot, video, scorecards, and background checks |
| 5 | Interview | Scheduling (and self-scheduling), kits, AI-generated questions |
| 6 | Make Offer | Templates, letters, predictive hiring, approvals |

Some groups have a single page; others have many. **Screen & Evaluate** has five pages (the screening config, plus four screening tools); **Make Offer** has five (templates → letter → predictive prior → approvals). Don't try to learn them all on day one — Chapters 5–9 walk through them in order.

![[c-ch045-sidebar-hiring-path.png]]

## The five Parallel Tracks

Below the divider you'll find:

- **Vendor & Sourcing** (3 pages) — Vendor Management, Talent CRM, AI Search. You'll touch this when you're working with external recruitment agencies or sourcing beyond inbound applications. Most TA teams reach here in Week 2 or 3.
- **Communication & Automation** (5 pages) — Unified Inbox, Email Templates, Brand Voice, Content Repurpose, Approvals. You'll start using this on Day 2 once your first candidates are landing.
- **Compliance & Governance** (4 pages) — POPIA, B-BBEE, Employment Equity, Audit Trail. You'll touch these monthly for routine reporting and during audits.
- **Workforce & HR** (7 pages) — HRIS Integration, Workforce Planning, Internal Job Board, Performance, Succession Planning, Contract Workers, Team DNA. The pages that connect hiring to the rest of HR — most relevant to TA leaders, HRBPs, and CHROs.
- **Analytics** (4 pages) — Recruitment Analytics, Predictive Analytics, Diversity Analytics, Custom Reports. You'll start using this in Week 2 when you have data to look at.

The Parallel Tracks are not optional, but they don't need attention every day. They activate at specific moments — when an audit lands, when time-to-fill creeps up, when EE reporting is due, when a hiring manager pushes back on calibre.

![[c-ch045-sidebar-parallel-tracks.png]]

## Supporting

At the bottom you'll find:

- **Knowledge Base** — your own internal wikis (notes, processes, playbooks for your TA team). Different from Sebenza Hub's product documentation.
- **Account** — Billing, Settings, SSO Config. The admin surface — set up once, revisited rarely.

You can ignore Knowledge Base on day one. The Account group you've already touched in Chapters 3 and 4.

![[c-ch045-sidebar-supporting-account.png]]

## What the dashboard actually shows you

Two things are wired into the dashboard from day one — one tells you how the function is performing, the other tells you what you're spending. Both are free on every plan. Get familiar with both before you start posting requisitions.

### Basic Analytics — _Tier: Free_

**What this feature is.** Basic Analytics shows requisition views, applicant counts, and the headline recruitment metrics at the requisition and organisation level. It's the always-on summary block on your Overview page — no setup required, populated automatically as activity flows in.

**Why it matters.** Every TA leader wants to know "how's the function performing?" without opening a separate analytics page. Free basics anchor that question inside Sebenza Hub instead of pushing you to spreadsheets — which is the only way the daily check-in habit forms. Deeper analytics (time-to-hire, funnel conversion, source-of-hire, cost-per-hire) are covered in [[01 How-To Documents/corporate/14-analytics-and-reporting|Chapter 14]].

**How to use it.** Runs automatically. Open [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/home](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/home) and the metrics tiles are at the top. Click any tile to drill into the underlying requisition or applicant list.

![[c-ch045-overview-metrics-tiles.png]]

### Billing & Usage Limits — _Tier: Free (all paying users)_

**What this feature is.** Billing & Usage Limits shows your current plan, invoices, payment methods, and — most importantly — real-time consumption of every metered feature against your monthly cap. Everything commercial about your account lives at [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/billing](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/billing), in the **Account** sidebar group.

**Why it matters.** No one wants a surprise bill. Transparent usage-vs-limit tracking is what stops finance from hitting a paywall in the middle of a hiring sprint and what tells you when an upgrade would save money compared to credit top-ups. [[01 How-To Documents/corporate/04-trial-and-billing-lifecycle|Chapter 4]] explains the commercial model in detail; this is where you watch it.

**How to use it.** Bookmark [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/billing](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/billing) from day one. Check the *Usage history* tab weekly during your first month so you learn which metered features your team actually consumes. The *Plan recommendation* card flags when your usage pattern suggests an upgrade.

![[c-ch045-billing-usage-page.png]]

## Notifications — set them once, on day one

The dashboard is also where you tell Sebenza Hub how to reach you when something needs your attention. Configure notification channels now so interview reminders, offer deadlines, candidate replies, and approval requests never sit in a tab someone forgot to open.

### Email Notifications — _Tier: Free_

**What this feature is.** Email Notifications send alerts and updates to recipients' inboxes — new applications, status changes, approval requests, scheduling events, candidate replies. The default channel for every account.

**Why it matters.** Email is the baseline notification channel everyone expects. Free access ensures no recruiter, hiring manager, or HRBP misses a critical update — interview time, offer acceptance, candidate decline — just because of the plan tier.

**How to use it.** Open Settings: [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/settings](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/settings) → **Notifications** and toggle the event types you want emailed. Defaults are sensible; tighten or loosen per your team's inbox tolerance.

### SMS Notifications — _Tier: Standard_

**What this feature is.** SMS Notifications deliver time-critical alerts to mobile — interview reminders, offer deadlines, 2FA codes, urgent approval pushes.

**Why it matters.** SMS has a ~98% open rate and reaches you without data. For time-critical moments (interview in 2 hours, an offer awaiting board-required approval, a candidate accepted overnight), SMS is the only channel that reliably gets through — and the same applies when SMS goes out to candidates on your behalf.

**How to use it.** In **Notifications**, add a verified mobile number per team member and tick the events you want via SMS. Keep the list short — every channel deserves a deliberate choice, not a check-everything default.

### All Notification Channels — _Tier: Premium_

**What this feature is.** Full channel coverage — email, SMS, push, and WhatsApp — with per-event channel rules. You can route candidate messages to WhatsApp, offer deadlines to SMS, and dashboard digests to email all from one config.

**Why it matters.** Different stakeholders prefer different channels, and a TA function on a busy day needs every channel available to reach hiring managers, candidates, and approvers. Multi-channel routing is the power-user setting that turns notifications from noise into signal.

**How to use it.** In **Notifications**, switch the view to **Per-event channels** and pick the channel for each event type. Set quiet hours to suppress non-urgent channels overnight. Per-user overrides let a hiring manager who lives in email opt out of SMS without affecting anyone else.

## Your first 30 minutes

Don't try to use everything. Here's the right order for your first half-hour after Chapter 3:

1. **Stay on Overview** — [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/home](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/home) — and skim the empty metrics tiles. They'll fill in as activity flows. (2 minutes)
2. **Open Team** — [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/team](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/team) — and confirm everyone you invited in Chapter 3 has accepted. Add the first hiring manager you'll be working with if you haven't already. (5 minutes)
3. **Go to Jobs** — [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/jobs](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/jobs) — and create one requisition draft (5 minutes). Don't publish it yet — just see how the form works. [[01 How-To Documents/corporate/05-posting-jobs|Chapter 5]] walks through this properly. Save as draft.
4. **Open Pipeline** — [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/pipeline](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/pipeline) — (2 minutes). It will be empty. That's fine. This is where you'll spend most of your daily time once applications start flowing in. [[01 How-To Documents/corporate/06-receiving-applications|Chapter 6]] explains the Kanban view.
5. **Visit Settings** — [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/settings](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/settings) — (5 minutes) and check who's listed as a team member, what notifications are on, and whether your time zone is correct.
6. **Bookmark Billing → Usage history** — [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/billing](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/billing) — so you can watch consumption against your trial caps. (1 minute)

That's it. Don't sign up for AI Search, don't connect HRIS, don't run a WhatsApp campaign. None of that is useful until your team, requisitions, and at least one real applicant are in the system.

## AI progress messaging — what those status updates mean

When you trigger an AI feature (screening a CV, generating a JD, summarising a candidate, scoring offer-acceptance probability), you'll see a multi-phase status indicator instead of a generic spinner: typically **drafting → validating → scoring → finalising**, with the active phase highlighted. This isn't decorative — it tells you what the platform is actually doing on your behalf.

Two practical effects:

- **You know whether it's stuck.** A spinner that hasn't moved in 30 seconds could be doing real work or could be hung. The phase indicator makes the difference visible — if you've been on "validating" for two minutes, that's a real problem worth reporting; if it's moved through three phases, it's just a slow run.
- **Feature chapters don't need to repeat it.** Every AI feature in this manual uses the same progress pattern. When [[01 How-To Documents/corporate/07-screening-and-evaluating|Chapter 7]] says "AI screening runs against every application", or [[01 How-To Documents/corporate/09-making-offers|Chapter 9]] says "Predictive Hiring scores acceptance probability", that's the same drafting → validating → scoring → finalising flow.

## Two patterns to watch out for

1. **The "shiny tools" trap.** Sebenza Hub has Predictive Hiring, Predictive Analytics, Team DNA, AI Search, Diversity Analytics, and a dozen other AI features. They're all useful — but they're useful *after* you have data to feed them. On day one, they have nothing to predict. Resist the urge to enable them all immediately. [[01 How-To Documents/corporate/19-advanced-and-ai-orchestration|Chapter 19]] covers the advanced surface once you have a baseline.
2. **The "everything at once" trap.** New customers sometimes try to set up HRIS sync, internal mobility, succession planning, brand voice, and the WhatsApp integration all in their first session. None of this works without real requisitions in the system. Build the Hiring Path first; layer the Parallel Tracks on top once it's flowing.
