# Chapter 3.5 — Quick Start: your first 3 actions

You've finished organisation setup (Chapter 3) — team invited, RBAC settled, branding stub in place, notifications configured. The dashboard at [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/home](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/home) is laid out in three bands: a **Hiring Path** (Organization → Post Jobs → Receive → Screen → Interview → Make Offer), **Parallel Tracks** (Vendor & Sourcing, Communication, Compliance, Workforce & HR, Analytics), and a **Supporting** band at the bottom (Knowledge Base and Account). The rest of the manual works through each group in detail.

Before you read another chapter, do these three things. They take about ten minutes and give your account a real shape — a requisition in the system, the pipeline ready to receive applications, and the billing page bookmarked so the trial doesn't surprise you.

> **What you'll have at the end:** one requisition saved as a draft, the Pipeline open and bookmarked, and the Billing page bookmarked.
>
> **What you won't have yet:** SSO, HRIS sync, vendor management, EE reports, internal mobility, succession planning, or any spend. That's deliberate — get a real requisition shape in the system first, then layer the enterprise plumbing on top in the chapters that follow.

## The three actions

### 1. Save one requisition draft (5 minutes)

Open the Jobs page: [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/jobs](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/jobs)

(Or click **Post Jobs** in the sidebar.)

Click **New Requisition** and fill in only the basics:

- Job title
- Department / hiring manager (your name is fine for now)
- Location (and remote / hybrid / onsite)
- A short job description — paste in any existing JD, or click the AI JD Writer to see what it generates from a few prompts
- **Save as draft.** Don't publish yet.

A draft requisition is enough to make every downstream feature (pipeline, screening, analytics) start showing realistic empty states instead of system defaults — which makes the rest of the manual much easier to follow when you read it.

The full requisition flow with screening criteria, distribution channels, EE targets, and approval routing is in [[01 How-To Documents/corporate/05-posting-jobs|Chapter 5]].

### 2. Open the Pipeline (2 minutes)

Open the Pipeline: [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/pipeline](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/pipeline)

It will be empty. That's fine. This is where every applicant for every requisition will land, organised by stage — the daily workspace for your TA team. Bookmark it now so you don't have to hunt for it later.

The full pipeline-management workflow is in [[01 How-To Documents/corporate/06-receiving-applications|Chapter 6]].

### 3. Bookmark the Billing page (3 minutes)

Open Billing: [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/billing](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/business/billing)

You're on a 30-day Premium trial right now. The Billing page shows you exactly when it expires, what your usage looks like across metered features, and how to convert before it ends. **Bookmark it.** Set a calendar reminder for day 23 of the trial to come back here and decide whether to convert. The biggest avoidable mistake corporate accounts make is letting the trial expire mid-evaluation without the finance contact noticing.

The full trial and billing lifecycle — what's metered, when reminders fire, how to convert without losing access — is in [[01 How-To Documents/corporate/04-trial-and-billing-lifecycle|Chapter 4]].

## Stop here

You're done. Requisition draft, Pipeline bookmarked, Billing bookmarked. About ten minutes.

Before you go any further:

- **Don't** wire up SSO yet. SSO works best when your roles and team are settled, and SSO setup involves your IT team — not something to do in your first session. [[01 How-To Documents/corporate/03-setting-up-your-org|Chapter 3]] covers it properly when you're ready.
- **Don't** wire up HRIS sync yet (SuccessFactors, Workday, BambooHR, Sage HR). Get one or two real hires through the platform first so you can see the data shape before you sync it. [[01 How-To Documents/corporate/13-workforce-and-hr|Chapter 13]] covers it.
- **Don't** start adding agencies as vendors yet. Vendor management gets its own chapter for a reason — it's a structured workflow involving POs, performance, and compliance. [[01 How-To Documents/corporate/10-vendor-management-and-sourcing|Chapter 10]] is the right starting point when you're ready.
- **Don't** worry about EE reports, B-BBEE, or POPIA configuration in your first session. They activate at specific moments — quarterly reporting, audits, candidate data export requests. [[01 How-To Documents/corporate/12-compliance-and-audit-trail|Chapter 12]] covers them.
- **Don't** convert your trial yet. You have 30 days. Use the platform first, then decide. [[01 How-To Documents/corporate/04-trial-and-billing-lifecycle|Chapter 4]] explains the lifecycle.
