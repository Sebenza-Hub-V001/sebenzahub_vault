# Quick Start — 10 minutes to your first job posted

The full Recruiter manual has 34 chapters and the recruiter dashboard has roughly 100 pages. You don't need any of that on day one. This page gives you the **bare minimum** — three actions that take ten minutes and get the platform from "empty shell" to "real agency with a real job".

If you only ever do these three things, your account is technically functional. Everything else is optimisation. Come back to the full manual when you want to do recruitment *well*, not just at all.

> **What you'll have at the end:** a basic agency profile, one client added, and one job posted as a draft.
>
> **What you won't have yet:** AI sourcing, screening automation, WhatsApp campaigns, analytics, branded career sites, or any spend. That's deliberate — get the bones in place first, then layer the power tools on top.

## The three steps

### 1. Sign up and fill in agency profile basics (4 minutes)

Sign up and pick **Recruiter** as your role. You'll land on `/dashboard/recruiter/profile`.

Fill in only the basics on the profile page:

- Agency name
- Logo (drag in any image — you can replace it later)
- A one-paragraph "About" describing what your agency does
- Your primary industry and the regions you cover

That's enough to look credible. The full rich-profile walkthrough — DNA Card, Reputation Score, Trust Tier, sector specialisations, performance widgets — is in [[01 How-To Documents/recruiter/05-agency-profile|Chapter 5]] and [[01 How-To Documents/recruiter/06-ai-profile-assets|Chapter 6]]. Skip them for now.

> ⚠️ **Your role is locked after signup.** Recruiter stays Recruiter. If you also need to test the candidate experience or run a corporate account, sign up with a different email. [[01 How-To Documents/recruiter/02-creating-your-account|Chapter 2]] covers the implications.

### 2. Add at least one client (2 minutes)

Click **Clients** in the sidebar → **Add Client**.

Enter:

- Client company name
- One contact person and their email
- (Optional) Industry and a brief note about the relationship

If you're a solo agency doing direct hires for yourself, add yourself as the client — the system needs at least one client before a job has commercial meaning.

The full client-management workflow — engagement terms, fee structures, contracts, satisfaction tracking — is in [[01 How-To Documents/recruiter/08-adding-clients|Chapter 8]] through [[01 How-To Documents/recruiter/10-managing-client-relationships|Chapter 10]]. Don't fill any of that in yet.

### 3. Create one job draft (4 minutes)

Click **Post Jobs** in the sidebar → **New Job**.

Pick the client you just added, give the job a title, paste in any job description (or use the AI JD Writer button if you'd rather see what it does), pick a location, and **save as draft**. Don't publish it yet.

That's it — your account now has a real shape. The full job-posting flow with AI JD Writer, screening criteria, and distribution is in [[01 How-To Documents/recruiter/11-posting-a-job|Chapter 11]].

## Stop here

You're done. Profile, client, job. Ten minutes.

Before you go any further:

- **Don't** turn on AI Search, LinkedIn Lead Import, or Talent Discovery yet. They need a real job posted (not just a draft) and a baseline of activity before they're useful. [[01 How-To Documents/recruiter/12-sourcing-channels|Chapter 12]] covers them properly.
- **Don't** set up WhatsApp campaigns, sequences, or the chatbot yet. None of it works without real candidates in the pipeline. [[01 How-To Documents/recruiter/22-unified-inbox|Chapter 22]] through [[01 How-To Documents/recruiter/24-whatsapp-at-scale|Chapter 24]] explain when and how.
- **Don't** enable Predictive Hiring, Video Analysis, Offer Predictor, or any of the other AI dashboards yet. They need data to predict from. [[01 How-To Documents/recruiter/34-advanced-and-platinum|Chapter 34]] covers the advanced surface once you have a baseline.
- **Don't** worry about Compliance, Brand & Growth, or Analytics in your first session. They activate at specific moments — when an audit lands, when you're trying to win a new client, when fill rates drop.
