# Chapter 3.5 — Quick Start: your first 3 actions

You've finished agency onboarding (Chapter 3). Your organisation exists, your basic agency record is in the system, and you're sitting on the dashboard at [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/recruiter/profile](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/recruiter/profile) looking at a sidebar with roughly 100 pages.

The full Recruiter manual has 34 chapters covering all of those pages. You don't need any of that today. This page gives you the **bare minimum** — three actions that take ten minutes and get the platform from "I just signed up" to "real agency with a real job in flight". If you only ever do these three things, your account is functional. Everything else — AI sourcing, screening automation, WhatsApp campaigns, branded career sites, analytics — is optimisation that comes later.

Once you've finished, Chapter 4 walks you through the rest of the dashboard layout, and the rest of the manual goes deeper feature by feature.

> **What you'll have at the end:** one client added, one job posted as a draft, and the Pipeline open and ready for candidates.
>
> **What you won't have yet:** AI sourcing, screening automation, WhatsApp campaigns, analytics, branded career sites, or any spend. That's deliberate — get the bones in place first, then layer the power tools on top.

## The three actions

### 1. Add at least one client (3 minutes)

Open the Clients page: [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/recruiter/clients](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/recruiter/clients)

(Or click **Clients** in the sidebar.)

Click **Add Client** and enter:

- Client company name
- One contact person and their email
- (Optional) Industry and a brief note about the relationship

If you're a solo agency doing direct hires for yourself, add yourself as the client — the system needs at least one client before a job has commercial meaning.

The full client-management workflow — engagement terms, fee structures, contracts, satisfaction tracking — is in [[01 How-To Documents/recruiter/08-adding-clients|Chapter 8]] through [[01 How-To Documents/recruiter/10-managing-client-relationships|Chapter 10]]. Don't fill any of that in yet.

### 2. Create one job draft (5 minutes)

Open the Jobs page: [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/recruiter/jobs](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/recruiter/jobs)

(Or click **Post Jobs** in the sidebar.)

Click **New Job**. Pick the client you just added, give the job a title, paste in any job description (or use the AI JD Writer button if you'd rather see what it does), pick a location, and **save as draft**. Don't publish it yet.

The full job-posting flow with AI JD Writer, screening criteria, and distribution is in [[01 How-To Documents/recruiter/11-posting-a-job|Chapter 11]].

### 3. Open the Pipeline (2 minutes)

Open the Pipeline: [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/recruiter/pipeline](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/recruiter/pipeline)

It will be empty. That's fine. This is where you'll spend most of your daily time once candidates start flowing in — the Kanban board with every candidate at every stage. Bookmark it now so you don't have to hunt for it later.

The full pipeline-management workflow is in [[01 How-To Documents/recruiter/16-the-pipeline|Chapter 16]].

## Stop here

You're done. Client, job draft, pipeline bookmarked. Ten minutes.

Before you go any further:

- **Don't** turn on AI Search, LinkedIn Lead Import, or Talent Discovery yet. They need a real job posted (not just a draft) and a baseline of activity before they're useful. [[01 How-To Documents/recruiter/14-sourcing-channels|Chapter 14]] covers them properly (with Chapter 13 covering LinkedIn Leads and Talent Rediscovery).
- **Don't** set up WhatsApp campaigns, sequences, or the chatbot yet. None of it works without real candidates in the pipeline. [[01 How-To Documents/recruiter/22-unified-inbox|Chapter 22]] through [[01 How-To Documents/recruiter/24-whatsapp-at-scale|Chapter 24]] explain when and how.
- **Don't** enable Predictive Hiring, Video Analysis, Offer Predictor, or any of the other AI dashboards yet. They need data to predict from. [[01 How-To Documents/recruiter/34-advanced-and-platinum|Chapter 34]] covers the advanced surface once you have a baseline.
- **Don't** worry about Compliance, Brand & Growth, or Analytics in your first session. They activate at specific moments — when an audit lands, when you're trying to win a new client, when fill rates drop.
