# Chapter 3.5 — Quick Start: your first 3 actions

You've finished onboarding (Chapter 3). You're sitting on the dashboard at [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/individual/profile](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/individual/profile) and the sidebar has dozens of pages. The full Individual manual has 24 chapters. You don't need any of that right now.

This page gives you the **bare minimum** — three actions that take five minutes and get you the platform's most useful first signal: a free AI review of your CV. If you only ever do these three things, you've already done more than most users on day one.

Once you've finished, Chapter 4 walks you through the rest of the dashboard, and the rest of the manual goes deeper feature by feature.

> **What you'll have at the end:** a CV uploaded to your profile, an AI review of that CV with a score and a few concrete improvements, and a sense of what jobs the platform already thinks fit you.
>
> **What you won't have yet:** any job applications, any saved searches, any messages, anything paid. That's deliberate — get the signal first, then decide where to invest.

## The three actions

### 1. Upload a CV (2 minutes)

Open **My CVs** — click it in the left sidebar (under *Build Profile*), or go directly to: [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/individual/cvs](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/individual/cvs)

Click **Upload CV** and drop in any existing CV — Word or PDF, in whatever shape it's in. Don't rewrite it first. The AI is going to tell you what to fix; uploading the messy version is the point.

The full upload walkthrough — including the in-platform CV Builder if you don't have a CV to upload — is in [[01 How-To Documents/individual/05-uploading-a-cv|Chapter 5]].

### 2. Run a CV Review (2 minutes)

Open the CV Review page directly: [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/individual/cv-review](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/individual/cv-review)

(Or click **CV Review** in the sidebar under *Build Profile*.)

Pick the CV you just uploaded and click **Run Review**. It takes 30–60 seconds. You'll see a multi-tab dashboard with up to 14 different analyses — overall score, ATS friendliness, keyword fit, formatting, missing sections, and more. Read the **Overall Score** and the top three improvement suggestions. That's it for now.

The full breakdown of every tab and what each score means is in [[01 How-To Documents/individual/06-cv-review|Chapter 6]].

### 3. Glance at Auto Match (1 minute)

Open **Auto Match**: [https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/individual/auto-match](https://www.sebenzahub.co.za/dashboard/individual/auto-match)

Don't apply to anything. Just scroll the top 5–10 matches and read the match scores. With a fresh profile your matches will be sparse — that's normal. The point is to see what the platform thinks fits you *today* so you have something to compare against once you've fixed the issues your CV review flagged.

The full search modes (All Jobs, Auto Match, Manual Search) are covered in [[01 How-To Documents/individual/09-search-modes|Chapter 9]].

## Stop here

You're done. You've got a CV, a real piece of feedback on it, and a sense of where the platform thinks you fit. Five minutes.

Before you go any further:

- **Don't** start applying to jobs yet. Recruiters typically filter for a complete profile, verified skills, and a polished CV before they shortlist. Spending an hour fixing the issues your CV review just flagged will get you better responses than blasting out fifty rushed applications. [[01 How-To Documents/individual/05-uploading-a-cv|Chapter 5]] and [[01 How-To Documents/individual/07-skills-credentials-video|Chapter 7]] are where you do that.
- **Don't** turn on Autopilot, Career DNA, or Market Radar yet — they need data and a complete profile to be useful. Part 6 of the manual covers them once you're ready.
- **Don't** upgrade your plan yet. The Free tier covers everything in this quick start and most of what you need to start job-hunting. Upgrade only when you hit a specific limit you actually care about.
